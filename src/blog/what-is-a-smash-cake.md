A smash cake is a small cake made just for the birthday baby at a first birthday party. The idea is simple: set it in front of the baby and let them dig in with their hands. It makes for some of the best photos of the day.

## How big should a smash cake be?

A smash cake only needs to be big enough for one small person to explore, so it's much smaller than a party cake.

## Tips for a happy smash

- **Match the party.** Laura can design the smash cake and party cake together in your colors and theme.
- **Think about timing.** Schedule the smash between naps if you can.
- **Dress for the mess.** A bib, or a simple outfit you don't mind getting frosting on, is a good idea.
- **Plan for photos.** Natural light and a plain background help the cake and the baby stand out.
