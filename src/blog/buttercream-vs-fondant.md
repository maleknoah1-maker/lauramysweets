Buttercream and fondant are the two most common ways to finish and decorate a cake. They look, taste and behave differently, and the right one depends on your design.

## Buttercream

Buttercream is a soft, creamy frosting spread and piped onto the cake. It can be smooth, textured, piped into borders and ruffles, or painted with colorful flowers, like Laura's three-tier painted flower cake.

- **Taste:** rich and creamy
- **Look:** soft edges, textures and piping
- **Best for:** most birthday cakes, vintage-style cakes, textured and ombre designs

**All of Laura's cakes are vanilla buttercream on the outside.** You still get to choose the cake flavor and filling inside. See the [flavors and fillings](/menu/#flavors).

## Fondant

Fondant is a smooth sugar dough that's rolled out and shaped. It holds crisp edges and detailed shapes, which makes it popular for figures, toppers and decorations.

- **Taste:** sweet, with a firmer, chewier texture
- **Look:** sharp lines, sculpted shapes and figures
- **Best for:** toppers, characters, lettering and detailed decorations

## Which should you choose?

If you want a creamy, classic cake with piping or texture, buttercream is the way to go. If your design needs shaped figures or crisp details, ask about adding fondant decorations.
